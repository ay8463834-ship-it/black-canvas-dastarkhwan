Our story

A dastarkhwan is a royal spread. Ours has fed Lucknow for decades.

From the first floor of the Novelty Cinema building in Kaiserbagh, The Mughal's Dastarkhwan keeps the courtly kitchen of Awadh alive: slow fires, whole spices, and recipes handed down rather than written down. Families come for Sunday nihari. Travellers come because their grandparents told them to.

On the table

The classics, done the slow way.

Galouti Kebab · house legend

Minced fine enough to be eaten without teeth, seared on the griddle, served with warqi paratha.

Nihari · overnight

Slow-simmered till the gravy coats the spoon, best eaten before the lunch rush finds it.

Haleem · winter special

Pounded wheat and meat cooked down for hours, dressed with fried onion and lemon.

Awadhi Biryani · dum style

Fragrant, layered, sealed and steamed. Lucknow's version is about delicacy, not heat.
